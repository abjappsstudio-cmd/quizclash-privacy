QuizClash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

QuizClash (“the app”) is a quiz game for Android published by AJ Apps Studio (“we”, “us”). It is made for players of all ages, including children aged 5 and up. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you and your parents have. If you are a child, please read it with a parent or guardian.

In short: the app asks your age first. If you are under 13, the app switches to kids’ mode: it shows only ads meant for children, gives you a generated nickname instead of a typed name, never stores a photo or an email address on your profile, does not use Google sign-in, has no friends list or chat, and only matches you with other children. We do not sell personal information.

For children under 13, the online profile contains only: an anonymous account ID, the generated nickname, scores, level and league data. It does not contain an email address, a photo or a real name. We do not use analytics or usage tracking in the app.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The app has no chat or free-text messaging between players and does not process payments.

2. How we use information

We use the information to run the game, show leaderboards and let you play duels, keep your progress, show ads, keep the app secure, and find and fix crashes. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use children’s information for anything other than running the game.

3. What other players can see

Your display name, level, score and league standing can be seen by other signed-in players, for example on leaderboards and during duels. Your email address is never shown to other players. Children under 13 are matched in duels and leagues only with other children, and appear to others only as a generated nickname.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics) for sign-in, data storage and crash reports. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In, only for players 13 and over who choose it. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob to display ads, including rewarded ads. policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform to ask for ad consent where the law requires it.
  • Google Play services, including in-app review prompts.

For players 13 and over, some quiz questions may be loaded from a public online trivia database. Players under 13 only get questions bundled inside the app.

5. Advertising

The app shows ads. Players under 13 (or whose age is not yet known) get ads requested with “child-directed treatment”: only ads suitable for children, no personalised ads, no interstitial (full-screen) ads between rounds, and no ad interest tracking. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

For players 13 and over, ads may be personalised unless you opt out. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings (“Ad privacy options”). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Children

We take children’s privacy seriously and design the app to follow children’s privacy laws such as the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including its rules for children), the UK Children’s Code, and Google Play’s Families Policy.

  • The app asks the player’s age before anything else. In kids’ mode a child can play as a guest, which needs no email address or personal information. A child may also sign in with email; this must be a parent’s or guardian’s address, and a parent should set it up.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without a parent’s permission, and we collect no more than the game needs.
  • Links that leave the app are protected by a “grown-up check” for children.
  • A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete everything we hold about their child by emailing ajappsstudio@gmail.com, or can delete the account in the app (see section 8). If we learn that we hold personal information from a child without the permission the law requires, we will delete it.
  • A child’s age is stored only on the device. It cannot be raised again from inside the app; a parent can reset the app’s data in the phone’s settings to start over.

7. Data storage and security

Your profile and game data are stored in Google Firebase and are protected by access rules so that players can change only their own data. Data is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your profile and game data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself: open the app, go to Settings and tap “Delete my account”. This permanently removes your profile, scores, league standing and friends from our servers and deletes your sign-in account. You can also email ajappsstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my QuizClash account” (use the address you signed in with, or for a guest account tell us your nickname) and we will delete the data within 30 days. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for a limited time (currently up to 90 days) and contain no contact details. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device but not your online profile.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, Brazil’s LGPD and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Parents and guardians can use these rights on behalf of their child. To use any of them, contact us at the address below. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. International transfers

Our service providers, including Google, may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards for such transfers. By using the app you understand your information may be transferred to and stored in those countries.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be shown in the app or on this page.
