QuizClash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

QuizClash (“the app”) is a quiz game for Android published by AJ Apps Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The app does not process payments.

2. How we use information

We use the information to provide and operate the game, show leaderboards and let you play against friends and other players, keep your progress, show ads, keep the app secure, diagnose crashes, and improve quality. We do not sell your personal information.

3. What other players can see

Your display name, profile photo (if you signed in with Google), level, score and league standing can be seen by other signed-in players, for example on leaderboards, in friend search and during duels. Your email address is not shown to other players. Choose a display name that does not reveal personal details.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) for sign-in, data storage, analytics and crash reports. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In if you choose to sign in with Google. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob to display ads, including rewarded ads, which may use the advertising ID. policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play services, including in-app review prompts.

5. Advertising

The app shows ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Opting out does not remove ads; they just become less personalized.

6. Children

The app asks for your age. If you are under 13, or you skip the question, the app treats you as a child for advertising: ads are requested with child-directed treatment, personalization is limited and ad content is restricted to a family-friendly rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is described above. If you believe a child has given us personal information that should be removed, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data storage and security

Your profile and game data are stored in Google Firebase and are protected by access rules so that players can change only their own data. Data is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your profile and game data for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and the data linked to it, email us at ajappsstudio@gmail.com from the address you signed in with (or, for a guest account, tell us your display name and approximate sign-up date). Include the subject “Delete my QuizClash account”. We will delete the data we hold about you within 30 days, except where we must keep something to meet legal obligations or prevent abuse. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device but not your online profile.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

10. International transfers

Our service providers, including Google, may process data in countries other than your own. By using the app you understand your information may be transferred to and stored in those countries.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be shown in the app or on this page.
